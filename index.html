Rate each one from 1 (a little) to 5 (overflowing), split by giving and getting, then save your shelf as a picture. Not your thing? Skip it and the jar stays empty.

Step 1

What's your name?

It goes on the label at the top. A nickname is fine, or leave it blank.

Step 2

Pick your flavor

The darker shade fills the side where you do it. The lighter shade fills the side where it's done to you.

Step 3

Pick your shelf

Start small or go big. Your answers stay put if you switch.

Step 4

Fill your jars

Tap a number. Tap it again to empty the jar. Jars with two rows have a side for doing it and a side for having it done to you.

Step 5

Your jars

Fill at least one jar, then tap Save my jars to make your picture.

On a phone you can also press and hold the picture to save it.

Nothing leaves your device. Your answers are kept only in this browser.
